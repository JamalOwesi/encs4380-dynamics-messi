Why this spec

        
The speed loop runs under the micromouse's straight-line and turn profiles. If one wheel overshoots its speed
           and the other does not, the mouse yaws a little at every speed change and drifts towards a wall, so we keep
           the damping in the practical 0.6–0.8 range from the lecture. The settling time sets how closely the wheels follow
           a speed profile: it must be well below the time of one cell-to-cell move. The PWM command must stay inside
           0–100 %: once it saturates the loop is open, the two wheels stop matching, and the integrator winds up.
           Zero steady-state error keeps both wheels at the same speed on long straights.
