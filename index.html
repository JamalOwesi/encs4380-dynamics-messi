02 / UNDERSTAND THE PHYSICS

What the equations mean

These are the closed-form formulas used to draw the graphs, not approximations.

01 · STEP

A sudden change

y(t) = KA(1 − e−t/τ)

For a step input of size A = 1, the output approaches KA. After one τ, it is 63.2% of the way there; after three τ, about 95%.

02 · RAMP

A continuously rising input

y(t) = KA[t − τ(1 − e−t/τ)]

For a ramp x(t) = At with A = 1 unit/s, the output eventually trails the ideal scaled ramp by a time lag τ and a vertical error KAτ.

03 · ERROR

Exponential decay

E(t) = e−t/τ

The normalized transient error falls exponentially. On a logarithmic vertical axis it becomes a straight line; the slope of ln(E) against time is −1/τ.
